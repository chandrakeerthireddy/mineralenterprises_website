About Us

A Leading Force in Mineral Trading

Mineral Enterprises is a full-service mining and trading company specializing in the extraction, processing, and global distribution of industrial minerals. We combine deep quarry expertise with modern production lines to serve paint, plastics, paper, rubber, steel, and construction industries.

Inspired by industry leaders across Asia, we maintain rigorous quality control at every stage — from raw material selection through final packaging and export logistics.
