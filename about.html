About Mineral Enterprises

Building trusted partnerships in mineral mining, processing, and international trade since our founding.

Our Story

From Quarry to Global Markets

Mineral Enterprises was founded with a clear vision: to become a reliable global supplier of high-quality industrial minerals. Drawing on best practices from leading mining companies across Asia — including integrated operations models seen at YBM Vietnam and similar regional producers — we built a company that controls quality from the source.

Today, we operate processing facilities equipped with modern grinding, classification, and coating lines. Our products serve paint manufacturers, plastic compounders, paper mills, rubber producers, and steel plants across more than 25 export markets.

We believe in long-term partnerships. Every shipment reflects our commitment to uniform quality, transparent documentation, and responsive customer service.
