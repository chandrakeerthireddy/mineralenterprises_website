Quality & Certifications

Rigorous quality control at every stage — from raw material to finished product and export.

Our Commitment

Prestige · Quality · Value

Quality is the cornerstone of Mineral Enterprises. Modeled after industry leaders like YBM Vietnam — which maintains an ISO 17025:2017 accredited laboratory — we invest in testing infrastructure and trained personnel to ensure every batch meets specification.

Our Quality Control department operates continuously, monitoring raw material intake, in-process parameters, and finished product analysis. We provide Certificate of Analysis (COA) with every shipment.

Key parameters tested include whiteness, brightness, particle size distribution (PSD), oil absorption, moisture content, and chemical composition (CaCO₃ content, Fe₂O₃, SiO₂, etc.).

Process

Quality Control Workflow

Four-stage quality assurance from quarry to export container.

Raw Material Inspection

Quarry samples tested for purity, whiteness, and chemical composition before acceptance into production.

In-Process Monitoring

Continuous PSD and moisture checks during grinding, classification, and coating operations.

Finished Product Testing

Full COA generated per batch — whiteness, brightness, PSD, oil absorption, and chemical analysis.

Pre-Shipment Verification

Final inspection, packaging integrity check, and documentation review before export dispatch.

Testing Parameters

What We Measure

Whiteness & Brightness
