Our Services

End-to-end mineral solutions — from extraction and processing to global delivery.

What We Do

Integrated Mineral Solutions

Following the model of leading Asian mineral producers, we offer a complete value chain so partners can source with confidence.

01

Mining & Extraction

Responsible quarry development and mineral extraction from premium deposits. We select raw material sources for uniformity, purity, and long-term supply stability.

  • Limestone and calcium carbonate quarry operations
  • Iron ore sourcing from certified mining partners
  • Environmental compliance and sustainable practices
02

Processing & Manufacturing

Modern production lines for grinding, classification, coating, and packaging. Capacity designed for both bulk industrial orders and specialty grades.

  • Ball mill and classifier grinding systems
  • Stearic acid surface coating lines
  • Custom particle size distribution (PSD) control
  • In-house laboratory testing at every batch
03

International Trading

Global mineral trading desk connecting producers with buyers across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and beyond. Competitive pricing with transparent contract terms.

  • Long-term supply agreements (LTSA)
  • Spot and contract pricing models
  • Multi-product portfolio trading
  • Market intelligence and price advisory
04

Export Logistics & Documentation

Full export support from factory gate to destination port. We handle shipping coordination, customs documentation, and quality certificates.

  • FOB, CIF, and CFR Incoterms support
  • Container and bulk vessel shipments
  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) per shipment
  • Phytosanitary and origin documentation
05

Technical Support & Customization

Dedicated technical team to help customers optimize mineral grades for their specific applications — from filler loading ratios to coating formulations.

  • Application-specific grade recommendations
  • Sample provision and trial support
  • Technical data sheets and MSDS
  • Custom packaging and labeling
